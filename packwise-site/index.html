    Liters are often listed on the bag's tag or product page. Not sure? Pick the closest picture above.

    How to take the photo

    1. Open your bagSo the scan can see how deep it is.
    2. Set an everyday item beside itA water bottle, credit card or soda can works as the ruler.
    3. Step back and shoot straight onFit the whole bag and the item in the photo.

    The scan gives a close estimate. If your bag's tag lists its size, that's even better.

    Usually printed on a tag inside the bag or on the handle. Add the size if you know it, like "Samsonite Freeform 21 inch".

    This usually takes under a minute. Hang tight, no need to tap again.

    TALK

    Tell your suitcase more

    Add anything the itinerary doesn't show, or ask a question. Your suitcase updates the list as you go. To talk: tap the box, tap the microphone on your keyboard and speak. When you stop, it sends on its own and the suitcase answers out loud.

    Turn your sound on and flip off silent mode. For a more natural voice on iPhone, go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English, download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium, then reload this page.
